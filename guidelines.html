Design Guidelines

Simple rules for the goodwork. website. Keep it calm, clear, and personal.

Colors

Cream
#f8f7f2
Forest
#243c32
Lime
#e2f18d
Mist
#dce3dc
Sage
#636d64
Orange (About)
#e67e22

Usage: Cream background for most pages. Forest for the home hero. Lime for buttons and accents. Orange is currently used on the About page.

Typography

Heading — Inter 36px / medium
Body — Inter 18px / regular
Small / secondary — Inter 14px / sage

Font: Inter (Google Fonts). Fallbacks: system-ui, sans-serif.

Tone: Short sentences. Warm and clear. Avoid jargon.

Spacing & layout

Max content width: 1200px, centered.

Page padding: 1.5rem sides, 3rem vertical on main sections.

Section gaps: 2–2.5rem between major blocks.

Cards and forms use 12px / 6px border radius.

Components

Header: Sticky. Wordmark left, nav right. Dark variant on About.

Primary button (CTA): Lime background, forest text, 6px radius, 16px / 600 weight.

Get in touch

Links: Inherit color. Underline only when needed. Lime on dark/orange backgrounds.

Pages

Home — Forest hero + three promise columns.

Services — Three simple cards.

About — Full-page orange background, text only.

Contact — Short form (name, email, message).

Principles

1. Keep it simple — one clear idea per section.

2. Personal — speak like a small team, not a corporation.

3. Consistent — reuse the color tokens and Inter everywhere.

4. Accessible — enough contrast, clear labels, keyboard-friendly links and buttons.
